# Research Competition Profile

适用于科研创新竞赛、学生科研项目展示、技术创新挑战赛。此 profile 是通用设计，不代表任何具体比赛官方标准。

## Inputs

- 项目简介、研究问题、创新点；
- 方法/技术路线、实验设计、原始数据与结果；
- 对照/基线、局限、应用场景；
- 用户提供的比赛规则、评分细则和提交规范；
- 演讲时长、页数限制、PDF/PPTX 要求。

## Narrative

1. 问题与重要性
2. 现有方法与缺口
3. 核心假设/研究目标
4. 创新点
5. 方法与技术路线
6. 实验设计与验证
7. 关键结果
8. 对比与可靠性
9. 应用价值
10. 局限与下一步
11. 总结贡献

## Rubric mapping

If official rules are provided:

```yaml
rubric_id:
dimension:
weight:
official_description:
source_location:
related_slides: []
evidence_status: sufficient | partial | missing
```

Rules:

- preserve official weights and source locations;
- do not invent missing dimensions;
- missing evidence becomes a declared gap, not a confident claim;
- without official rules, use general checks and label them as non-official.

## Competition checks

- innovation is specific and comparable;
- method explains the claimed innovation;
- experiments validate the central claim;
- baselines/controls are visible;
- limitations and maturity are not hidden;
- judges outside the field can follow the story;
- timing and slide count match the brief;
- the final PDF matches the promised delivery format.
