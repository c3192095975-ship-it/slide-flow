# Synthetic Research Competition Brief

## Project
A low-power sensor scheduling method for a campus environmental monitoring prototype.

## Audience
Mixed technical and non-technical judges.

## Goal
Explain the problem, method, evidence, limitations and application value within 8 minutes.

## Delivery
- Authoring format: editable PPTX
- Delivery format: PDF
- Target: 9 slides

## Constraints
- No invented experiment results.
- All quantitative claims must be supplied by the user or marked as missing.
- Visuals must distinguish measured results from conceptual diagrams.
